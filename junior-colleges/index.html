What your college gets

Made for how a junior college actually runs.

Not a school ERP with the words changed. Years, combinations and board exams are built in.

  • Years and combinations

    1st Year and 2nd Year with MPC, BiPC, CEC, HEC, MEC and vocational groups, each with its own subjects and seats.

  • Admissions with seat control

    A seat-full warning at admission, so a combination is never over-filled by mistake.

  • College exams

    Unit, weekly, grand, quarterly, half-yearly, annual, pre-final and practical exams, with marks entry and results.

  • Board hall tickets and results

    See which board hall tickets are issued and handed over, and the released results with the pass rate.

  • Practical batches

    Set up practical exam batches and keep them apart from the theory exams.

  • Scholarships

    Track scholarship forms and the money still to come, next to the fees.

  • Attendance follow-up

    An alert for students absent three or more days in a row, so the right calls are made the same day.

  • Year-end path

    1st Year moves to 2nd Year, and 2nd Year students leave with their records kept.

  • Fees, accounts and apps

    Fee receipts, concessions, payment vouchers, plus parent and student logins on the phone.

Questions

Before you book a demo.

Does it handle MPC, BiPC, CEC, HEC and MEC?

Yes. You list your combinations with their subjects and seats for 1st Year and 2nd Year, and press Create. Vocational groups and your own groups can be added too. Nothing is ever removed when you change them.

Can it track board hall tickets and results?

Yes. The ERP tracks board hall tickets, which are issued and which are handed over, and shows released board results with a pass rate for the year.

Does it have college-style exams?

Yes. Unit tests, weekly tests, grand tests, quarterly, half-yearly, annual, pre-final and practical exams are ready to use, and you can add your own exam types.

Does it include fees and accounting?

Yes. Fees, receipts, concessions, scholarship tracking and the accounts section are the same proven modules as in our school ERP.

Can we ask for changes?

Yes. SVM EdTech stays open to the changes each college asks for. The live demo has a Suggest a change button, so you can tell us what you need while you try it.

How much does it cost?

The College plan starts at Rs 100 per student per year, with a yearly minimum of Rs 30,000, plus a one-time setup of Rs 50 per student (minimum Rs 15,000). It includes every module. Your final price is agreed in a written quote, and larger colleges can get a better rate. There are no hidden charges.

Is our data kept separate?

Yes. Every college gets its own app and its own database, never shared with another institution.
