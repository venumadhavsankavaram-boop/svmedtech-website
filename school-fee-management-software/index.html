What you get

Fees and accounts in one place.

  • Balances by student

Questions

Before you book a demo.

Does the fee module work with the school's accounts?

Yes. Fee receipts and balances are in the same system as payment vouchers and cost centres, so fees and accounts are not kept in two places.

Can parents see their child's fees?

Yes. Parents have their own login on a phone and see only their own child's fees and notices.

How are UPI payments handled?

A parent can report a UPI payment, and the school checks and verifies it before it counts.

How much does it cost?

It depends on your school's size and the package you choose. We quote after a free demo, and GST is extra.
