Questions schools ask

Before you book a demo.

Can we try it before we decide?

Yes. After the demo we can create a time-limited trial login for your school, with a login for each role (Admin, Staff and Parents), so your team can test every view.

Can schools outside Andhra Pradesh use it?

Yes. SVM EdTech is based in Andhra Pradesh, but it serves schools across India, in any state. We show the ERP in a live demo online, then set it up for your school.

Which boards does it work for?

Any. Grading scales, exam structure and report card layouts are set by your school, so State Board, CBSE and ICSE schools can all use it.

Can you move our existing student data in?

Yes. Student records can be imported from an Excel template, and we help you check them before your school goes live.

Is our data kept separate from other schools?

Yes. Every school gets its own app and its own database, and a full data backup can be downloaded at any time.

We already have a school website. Do we need the website package?

No. Choose ERP only. The website package is for schools that also want a new website in their own colours and logo.

Can parents and teachers use it on a phone?

Yes. Admin, staff and parents each get their own login and see only their own part. It installs like an app on a phone.

Does it handle fees and accounts together?

Yes. Fee receipts, balances, payment vouchers and cost centres sit in the same system, along with payroll including EPF and ESI.

How much does it cost?

Plans start at Rs 30 per student per year (Basic), Rs 45 (Core), Rs 70 (Complete) and Rs 85 (Complete + Website). Starter is free for up to 100 students. Each plan has a yearly minimum, and the prices are shown in the Pricing section above. GST is extra if it applies.

How does payment work? Why is there a setup charge?

There is a one-time setup fee and then a yearly fee. Setup covers creating your school's system, importing your students and fees, creating logins and training your staff. Setup and the first year are paid at signing. From the second year you pay only the yearly price.

Are there any hidden costs? Is GST included?

There are no hidden charges. Your written quote lists every line: setup, yearly fee and any extras. If GST applies, it is added on top and shown as a separate line on the bill. SMS, WhatsApp and online-payment gateway charges are at cost, only if you use them, and extra branches or customization are quoted before we start.

Can we start small and upgrade later?

Yes. Start on Basic and move up to Core or Complete when you need more. When you upgrade, only the yearly price changes. You do not pay a new setup charge for modules you already have.

Can teachers enter marks and attendance from their phones?

Yes, from the Core plan up. A teacher marks attendance and enters marks on the phone. When the teacher presses Final Submit, the marks are locked, and once an exam is released to parents the teacher cannot change them. Corrections go through a request to the Admin or Principal, with a reason and a record.

How are report cards and progress reports made?

Marks entered for each exam flow into report cards by themselves. You choose the weightage of each exam, such as FA and SA, and can add moderation marks for a subject. Progress reports can be taken at any stage of the year. A class sheet and each student's report can be printed, one by one or for the whole class.

How do fees work? Can parents pay online?

Fee heads, concessions, receipts and dues are all handled in the Fees module, and the office sees who has paid and who has not, class by class. Parents can pay by UPI from the app, and the office confirms the payment and issues the receipt. An online payment gateway can be added if the school wants it, with the gateway's charges paid at cost.

Does the ERP include accounting and payroll?

Yes, in the Complete plans. Accounting gives the Admin the school's income, expenses and vouchers in one place, and payroll handles staff attendance, leave and salary with EPF and ESI. Many school systems leave these out.

What about transport, hostel, library and inventory?

These modules are in the Complete plans. A school that does not need them can start on Core and upgrade later.

Is the school website really run from the ERP?

Yes, in the Complete + Website plan. The banner, notices, gallery, alumni, achievements and policy pages are edited in the ERP and appear on your website. Admission enquiries from the website come back into the ERP.

What if the server crashes? Can our data be restored?

Every school has automatic backups and a full backup you can download. We test restoring that backup into a fresh database on real school data. The Complete + Website plan includes managed off-server backups and restore help, within 24 hours of a crash.
