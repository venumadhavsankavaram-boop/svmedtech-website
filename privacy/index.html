Privacy

Privacy policy.

Last updated: 19 November 2026

This page explains what information SVM EdTech collects through this website and through the school software we supply, and how it is used. SVM EdTech is run by Venu Madhav Sankavaram, based in Andhra Pradesh, India. You can reach us at venu@svmedtech.com or on +91 77802 04177 (call or WhatsApp).

This website

You can ask for a demo or for the brochure by filling in a form. We collect only what you type there: your name, school name, phone or WhatsApp number, email and message. If you try our live demo, the start form collects your name, school name, WhatsApp number and, if you choose, the number of students. The demo uses only sample data and is open to you for three days; a small cookie on your device lets you return without filling the form again. This website does not use advertising trackers or analytics tools.

  • We use your details to reply to you, to send the brochure and to follow up about a demo.
  • We do not sell your details and we do not share them with advertisers.
  • Your details are stored in our own dashboard. Only the people who run SVM EdTech can see them.
  • We keep an enquiry for as long as we need to follow it up. You can ask us to correct or delete it at any time by writing to us.

The school software (ERP)

When a school uses our ERP, the school's records, such as students, parents, staff, fees, attendance and marks, belong to the school. The school decides what to enter and who may see it.

  • Separate for each school. Every school has its own app and its own database. One school's data is never mixed with another's.
  • Used only to run the service. We use a school's data only to set up, support and secure the software for that school, and to restore it from a backup if needed. We do not sell it or use it for advertising.
  • Who can see it. Each user has a login and a role, and sees only what that role allows. Changes to important records are written to a change log.
  • Backups. The software makes automatic backups, and the school can download a full copy. We may use a school's backup file to restore its system, only when the school asks or the service needs recovering.
  • Children's information. The software holds information about students. The school is responsible for informing parents and for any consent it needs. We handle that information only on the school's behalf.

Our service providers

We use hosting and infrastructure providers to run this website and the software. They store data on our behalf and are not allowed to use it for their own purposes.

Your choices

You may ask us to see, correct or delete the details we hold about you. For information held inside a school's ERP, please ask the school first, because the school controls that data. We will help the school with its request.

Changes to this page

If we change this page, we will update the date at the top. If you have a question, write to venu@svmedtech.com.
