What you get

Complete, simple and close to the builder.

  • Years and group combinations

    1st and 2nd Year with MPC, BiPC, CEC, HEC and MEC, sections and seat control at admission.

  • Board hall tickets and results

    Hall tickets and marks memos for board exams, with college exams and practical batches handled separately.

  • Scholarships and fees

    Scholarship tracking, fee collection with receipts, and accounts in the same system.
