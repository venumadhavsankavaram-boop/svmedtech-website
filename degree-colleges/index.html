What your college gets

Made for how a degree college actually runs.

Not a school ERP with the words changed. Programmes, semesters, credits and results are built in.

  • Programmes and years

    B.A., B.Com and B.Sc as 3-year Degree or 4-year Honours, each with its own seats, years and semesters.

  • Courses and credits

    Major, Minor, Elective, MDC, SEC, VAC, AEC, practical, internship and project courses, with credits you can edit.

  • Internal and external marks

    Enter marks per course, internal and external, with the pass mark and maximums you set.

  • SGPA, CGPA and mark sheets

    Credits earned, SGPA per semester and CGPA so far, backlogs, and a printable mark sheet.

  • Attendance and shortage

    Attendance percentage per student, and a list of students below your college's minimum.

  • Fee reimbursement tracking

    A clerk's sheet for applicants, certificate numbers, bank link and biometric status, and the files that still need fixing. It is not connected to any government portal.

  • Internship and community service

    Track which students still have an internship or community service open.

  • Your university, your rules

    Course lists, credits, grading scale, pass mark and attendance minimum are all editable to match your university.

  • Fees, accounts and apps

    Fee receipts, concessions, payment vouchers, plus faculty and student logins on the phone.

Questions

Before you book a demo.

Does it handle B.A., B.Com and B.Sc programmes?

Yes. You add each programme with its pattern (3-year Degree or 4-year Honours), seats and courses. Years and semesters are created for you, and you can edit every list.

Can it match our university's course and grading rules?

Yes. Course lists, credits, internal and external marks, the pass mark, the minimum attendance and the grade table are all editable, because every university differs. We start you with common values and you change them to match your university.

How are results worked out?

Marks are entered per course, internal and external. The ERP works out each student's credits earned, SGPA for the semester and CGPA so far, shows backlogs, and prints a mark sheet.

Does it track fee reimbursement?

Yes, as a tracking sheet for the college clerk: who is applying, certificate numbers, the mother's bank link and biometric status, with a list of files that still need fixing. It does not connect to any government portal.

Does it include fees and accounting?

Yes. Fees, receipts, concessions and the accounts section are the same proven modules as in our school ERP.

What do faculty use?

Faculty log in on the phone or computer to mark attendance, enter marks and see their timetable. The ERP uses the word Faculty throughout for degree colleges.

How much does it cost?

The College plan starts at Rs 100 per student per year, with a yearly minimum of Rs 30,000, plus a one-time setup of Rs 50 per student (minimum Rs 15,000). It includes every module. Choose the 3-year price lock and the yearly fee is 10% lower (Rs 90 per student, minimum Rs 27,000) with the price fixed for 3 years; setup is not discounted. Your final price is agreed in a written quote, and larger colleges can get a better rate. There are no hidden charges.

Is our data kept separate?

Yes. Every college gets its own app and its own database, never shared with another institution.
