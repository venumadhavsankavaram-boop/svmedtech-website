Using this website

You may read this website and ask for a demo or brochure. Please give correct details and do not misuse the forms. The information here describes our software and is meant as a guide; your written quote is what applies to your school or college. Features described for a school, a junior college or a degree college apply to that type of institution.

Demo and trial

The live demos (school, junior college and degree college) use sample data. A trial login may be given for a limited time. Please do not enter real student, staff or fee information into a demo. Anything you type into a demo, including feedback and suggestions, may be read by us and used to improve our software.

Plans and prices

  • Prices for schools are shown on our pricing section, per student per year, with a one-time setup charge and a yearly minimum for each plan.
  • College plans for junior and degree colleges are Selected Modules (Rs 60 per student per year), Complete Modules (Rs 100) and Complete Modules with website (Rs 120), each with a yearly minimum and a one-time setup shown on the college pricing section. The final price for a college is the one written in its quote.
  • A 3-year price lock is available: the yearly fee is 10% lower in each of the 3 years and the price stays fixed for that term. The one-time setup fee is not discounted. The exact terms of a 3-year lock are written in your quote before you sign.
  • Setup is paid once. From the second year only the yearly price is paid.
  • GST is extra if it applies and is shown as a separate line on the bill.
  • SMS, WhatsApp and online payment gateway charges are billed at cost, only if the school uses them. Extra branches and work outside the plan are quoted before we start.
  • You receive a written quote that lists every line. Nothing is charged that is not in the quote.

Payment

Setup and the first year are paid at signing. After that the yearly fee is paid each year, before the new year begins.

Support and customization

Support, reply times, training and the number of customization requests depend on the plan, as shown in the service table on our pricing section. Reply times are counted in working hours. Larger changes and new modules are quoted separately.
