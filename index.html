Questions schools ask

Before you book a demo.

Is our data kept separate from other schools?

Yes. Every school gets its own app and its own database, and a full data backup can be downloaded at any time.

We already have a school website. Do we need the website package?

No. Choose ERP only. The website package is for schools that also want a new website in their own colours and logo.

Can parents and teachers use it on a phone?

Yes. Admin, staff and parents each get their own login and see only their own part. It installs like an app on a phone.

Does it handle fees and accounts together?

Yes. Fee receipts, balances, payment vouchers and cost centres sit in the same system, along with payroll including EPF and ESI.

Is the help available in Telugu?

Yes. The built-in Help Centre is in English and Telugu.

How does payment work?

There is a one-time setup fee and a yearly fee. Setup and the first year are paid at signing, then the yearly fee each year. You get the full quote after the demo, and GST is extra.

How much does it cost?

It depends on your school's size and which package or modules you choose. Message us and we will send a quote after the demo.
