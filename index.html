About SVM EdTech

Built by someone who has seen school ERPs up close.

SVM EdTech is run by Venu Madhav Sankavaram, Founder. For 15 years he has worked with school ERP systems, and kept meeting the same problems: products that feel half finished, changes that take months or never arrive, and no real accounts section for the management.

So he built one that covers the whole institution, from admissions to accounts, and stays open to the changes each one asks for. It started with schools and now serves junior colleges and degree colleges too. SVM EdTech is based in Andhra Pradesh and serves institutions across India, in any state. The demos are shown live online, so you can see and start from anywhere.
