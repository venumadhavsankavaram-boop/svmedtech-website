What you get

A school ERP for schools in Annamayya district.

  • Fees and accounts together

    Fee receipts, payment vouchers, cost centres and payroll sit in one system, so the office and the management see the same numbers.

  • Attendance for pupils and staff

    Mark attendance for pupils and staff, with optional biometric sync, and let parents see it on their phone.

  • Exams, marks and report cards

    Exam plans, marks entry, results, report cards and admit cards in one place.

  • A parent app on the phone

    Parents install it from the browser, with no store download, and see only their own child's fees, attendance and notices.

  • Choose only the modules you need

    A school can take the full ERP, or pick modules one by one, such as fees, accounting or attendance.

  • Your own app and database

    Each school gets a separate app, and a full data backup can be downloaded at any time.
