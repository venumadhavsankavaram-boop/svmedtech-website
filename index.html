Questions

What owners ask us first.

Can we try it before we decide?

Yes. After the demo we can create a time-limited trial login for your institution, with a login for each role (Admin, Staff and Parents), so your team can test every view.

How much does it cost?

Plans for schools start at Rs 3,000 a year for the Starter plan (up to 100 students, with free setup), and then Rs 30 per student per year for larger schools. College plans start at Rs 100 per student per year. The paid plans have a one-time setup fee and then a yearly fee. GST is extra if it applies, and there are no hidden charges: your written quote lists every line.

What is the 3-year price lock?

You agree to a 3-year term and your yearly fee is 10% lower in each of those years, with the price fixed for the whole term. The one-time setup fee is not discounted. The terms are written in your quote before you sign.

Can you move our existing student data in?

Yes. Student records can be imported from an Excel template, and we help you check them before you go live.

Is our data kept separate from other institutions?

Yes. Every institution gets its own app and its own database, and a full data backup can be downloaded at any time.

Is our data safe, and will there be any problem with the government if we use it?

The records belong to your institution: you decide what is entered and who can see it. Every institution has its own app and its own database, each person sees only what their role allows, important changes are written to a change log, and you can download a full backup whenever you want. We do not sell your data or use it for advertising. India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act asks schools and colleges to handle student and parent data carefully, including getting consent where needed. The ERP gives you the tools for that: role-based access, change logs, backups and deletion on request. We are not a law firm, so please confirm the details for your institution with your own adviser. Our privacy policy has the full wording. Read the privacy policy.

Does it handle fees and accounts together?

Yes. Fee receipts, balances, payment vouchers and cost centres sit in the same system, along with payroll including EPF and ESI. Accounting and payroll are part of the Complete plans.

Can parents and teachers use it on a phone?

Yes. Admin, staff and parents each get their own login and see only their own part. It installs on a phone like an app, with no app store needed.

Can institutions outside Andhra Pradesh use it?

Yes. SVM EdTech is based in Andhra Pradesh, but it serves schools and colleges across India, in any state. We show the ERP in a live demo online, then set it up for you.

What if we need a change made?

Ask us. Changes such as a report or receipt layout, fee heads, a custom export or a small screen change take a few hours and are made by the person who built the ERP. New modules are quoted separately.
