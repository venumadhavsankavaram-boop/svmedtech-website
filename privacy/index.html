Privacy

Privacy policy.

Last updated: 20 November 2026

This page explains what information SVM EdTech collects through this website and through the school and college software we supply, and how it is used. SVM EdTech is run by Venu Madhav Sankavaram, based in Andhra Pradesh, India. You can reach us at venu@svmedtech.com or on +91 77802 04177 (call or WhatsApp).

This website

You can ask for a demo or for the brochure by filling in a form. We collect only what you type there: your name, school or college name, phone or WhatsApp number, email and message. If you try one of our live demos (school, junior college or degree college), the start form collects your name, school or college name, WhatsApp number and, if you choose, the number of students. The demos use only sample data and are open to you for three days; a small cookie on your device lets you return without filling the form again. This website does not use advertising trackers or analytics tools.

Your choice of institution. The site remembers which kind of institution you looked at (school, junior college or degree college) in your own browser only, so it can show you the right page next time. The Choose another button on the home page, or clearing your browser data, removes it.

Feedback. A Feedback button appears on every page, and on a computer we may show one prompt as you are about to leave. In the live demos, signing out offers an optional feedback form too. Answering is optional. If you send feedback we receive your rating, the topics you pick, what you write, the page you were on and, only if you type them, your name and phone or email. We use it only to improve our software and to reply to you if you asked us to.

  • We use your details to reply to you, to send the brochure and to follow up about a demo.
  • We do not sell your details and we do not share them with advertisers.
  • Your details are stored in our own dashboard. Only the people who run SVM EdTech can see them.
  • We keep an enquiry for as long as we need to follow it up. You can ask us to correct or delete it at any time by writing to us.

The school and college software (ERP)

When a school or college uses our ERP, its records, such as students, parents or guardians, staff (teachers, lecturers or faculty), fees, attendance, marks and results, belong to that institution. The institution decides what to enter and who may see it.

  • Separate for each institution. Every school and college has its own app and its own database. One institution's data is never mixed with another's.
  • Used only to run the service. We use an institution's data only to set up, support and secure the software for that institution, and to restore it from a backup if needed. We do not sell it or use it for advertising.
  • Who can see it. Each user has a login and a role, and sees only what that role allows. Changes to important records are written to a change log.
  • Backups. The software makes automatic backups, and the institution can download a full copy. We may use an institution's backup file to restore its system, only when the institution asks or the service needs recovering.
  • Students' information. The software holds information about students, who may be children or adults. The school or college is responsible for informing students and parents or guardians and for any consent it needs. In a college this can include marks, attendance and records kept for scholarship or fee reimbursement, such as certificate or card numbers. The college decides what to keep, and we handle it only on its behalf.

Our service providers

We use hosting and infrastructure providers to run this website and the software. They store data on our behalf and are not allowed to use it for their own purposes.

Your choices

You may ask us to see, correct or delete the details we hold about you. For information held inside an institution's ERP, please ask the school or college first, because it controls that data. We will help it with your request.
