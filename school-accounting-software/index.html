Accounting

School accounting software, built in and not an afterthought.

Many school ERPs have no real accounts section for the management. SVM EdTech keeps fee receipts, payment vouchers, cost centres and payroll in the same system.

Book a free demo

Built and used in a live school, Raghavan EM High School, and available to other schools as their own separate app.

What you get

Accounts that live beside fees and payroll.

  • Fee receipts

    Fees collected flow into the same system as the rest of the school's accounts.

  • Payment vouchers

    Record what the school pays out, with a voucher for each payment.

  • Cost centres

    See income and spending by cost centre, such as a branch or a department.

  • Payroll with EPF and ESI

    Staff payroll, leave and loss of pay, with optional posting of salaries to Accounting.

  • Overview for management

    A view of the school's total position, so the management does not wait for a report.

  • Several branches

    Run several branches in one ERP. Branch staff see their own branch, and management can see all.

Questions

Before you book a demo.

Is accounting a separate product?

No. It is part of the same ERP as fees, payroll and the rest of the school.

Does payroll include EPF and ESI?

Yes. Payroll handles EPF and ESI, leave and loss of pay.

Can salaries post to Accounting?

Yes, as an optional setting.

Can the management see all branches?

Yes. Management can see all branches, and branch staff see their own.
