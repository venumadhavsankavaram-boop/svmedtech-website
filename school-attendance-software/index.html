Attendance

School attendance software for pupils and staff.

Mark attendance for pupils and for staff in one system, and let parents see it on their phone. Staff attendance connects to leave and payroll.

Book a free demo

Built and used in a live school, Raghavan EM High School, and available to other schools as their own separate app.

What you get

Attendance that connects to the rest of the school.

  • Pupil attendance

    Mark each class and see attendance by student without paper registers.

  • Staff attendance

    Track teachers and staff attendance in the same place.

  • Optional biometric sync

    If the school uses a biometric device, its attendance can be synced in. It is optional.

  • Parents see attendance

    Parents see their own child's attendance on their phone.

  • Leave and loss of pay

    Staff leave and loss of pay feed into payroll, including EPF and ESI.

  • Part of one system

    Attendance sits beside fees, exams, timetable and accounts, with one login per role.

Questions

Before you book a demo.

Does it cover both pupils and staff?

Yes. Attendance is kept for pupils and for staff.

Do we need a biometric device?

No. Biometric device sync is optional.

Can parents see attendance?

Yes. Parents log in on a phone and see only their own child's attendance.

Is it linked to payroll?

Yes. Staff leave and loss of pay feed into payroll, which handles EPF and ESI.
